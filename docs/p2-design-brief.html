PCJ-S P2 design brief (post review R1 + client decisions, 13.10.2026)

Build in CadQuery (engineering\.venv, cadquery 2.8). Library: src\cad\lib.py (EN 10219 RHS/SHS, plate, angle, Misumi 4590 profile verified 121.8 cm4 vs 124.7 cat., ISO fasteners, T-nut), src\cad\boughtin.py (latch, hinge, feet, cam lever, plunger, handwheel, ZIMM - jacks now unused). World: X along form (0 = left datum face), Y across (0 = Y datum = form near edge), Z up; face ZF = 850. Every part a real solid; every fastener placed.

Architecture

  1. Base frame (welded S355, ISO 13920 C/G, a3 fillets ISO 2553, shot-blast + powder coat, masking schedule): fixed bed top Z 400 (18 mm phenolic ply on the frame, flat 2 mm via shims). Side-bar posts WELDED to the frame with knee braces (first mode >= 75 Hz, LC7 300 N horizontal at bar top). End-bar portals = rigid welded frames on frame extensions. End-plate sub-frame + gate sub-frame + hinge pedestals + latch posts OUTSIDE the gate sweep. Lifting points/fork pockets. 8-10 x 500 kg levelling feet. Rivnuts / crush tubes for every bolt into RHS.
  2. Riser packs (one per series, colour band + label, dowel-located in the bed): bearers at riser height 850 - OW - 400 (Std12 12 ... Std4 215); extend Y -110..567 to carry EPS run-off strips beside the form.
  3. Datums: X = bolt-on machined datum plate on a welded bracket, shims, GD&T perp 0.2/457; hard pads LOW (below Z 700); replaceable EPS run-off block above. Y = 6082 flat-bar fence fixed to the frame, acetal pads every ~300, top <= Z 675 (below every cut band). Cut band = face to panel+15 (Std 768, XR35 733).
  4. End gate (right): light aluminium frame, spring stop <= 40 N (no squeeze), gas strut, detents flat/vertical, handle, hard pads low + EPS run-off block high; latches side-acting onto posts outside the sweep (>= 25 mm gaps).
  5. Hold: 3 far-side cam pushers on the bed frame (~30 N spring pads) push the form onto the Y fence.
  6. Bars: side = profile 4590 (or item 8 80x40 to 6 m), ONE dowelled bracket per bar at the X-datum end, others slotted with PTFE slip shims; Y dowelled +/-0.2. End bars raised: underside Z >= 950 (>= 60 over tallest stop). Tapes on a raised strip / outer land, zero set with a gauge off the datum face; brackets = flat plate + key, 11x31 slots, two variants, 4-point jacking. Goods-in straightness acceptance.
  7. Guides: 6082-T651 plate, Type II dyed anodise 10-15 um, slot 4 +0.2/0 (machine 4.05-4.15 pre-finish), straightness 0.3/1000 & 0.5 overall; 3x3 undercut both edges (stop + shoe hooks); tape recessed flush. V 900 x 124, A 1500 x 124, G 2800 x 144 (two 72 strips). Recalc C4 (panel over void + cut-through), C6 two-strip.
  8. Clamps (all with adjustable lens cursors, cams outboard, real cam levers + T-nuts): square head = fabricated 15 mm plate + fence, side-acting T-nut into the bar side slot + 2 top cams; V rest pad with cam; low pin clamp 300 long, spring plunger pin + cam gripping the guide (axial lock, hold-down); high pin clamp = puck + drop bracket + spring pin, 10 clearance; stop = side pinch on the undercut, flip lever, cursor offset per family (vertical blade -> exact at depth). Guide zero mark at the near pin.
  9. Saw: M12 Hackzall 2420 VERTICAL; printed shoe = depth-spacer body per panel family (Std / XR35) clipping the user's M12 cover, captive hooks on the guide undercut (no slot prong), D-handle, extraction port, bead brush. Blade 9 in toothed bi-metal 10 TPI (Milwaukee 48-00-5713). Spacer sets tip = panel + 15. Go/no-go blade gauge.
  10. Parking (walk-around, 0.9 m rear aisle): V left on side bars, A right on side bars, G on end bars toward the NEAR side with a park ledge at Z 850; A/G on sliding carriers (never lifted at reach). Park order defined.
  11. Flip station: padded flip trestle at the right end; X-end cuts at the gate end only; two-person SOP for cut forms.
  12. Process: cut-sheet generator spec (BCDS -> per-step settings, face 2 Y' = H_meas - Y, kerf to waste side, web warnings); web-severing step + tool; measure 10 forms/series before FAT.
  13. Deliverables: production BOM (PN per item, qty/assy, parent, make/buy, dwg no., rev, mass), fastener schedule, STEP per part + assembly, DXF, STL, PDF drawings from the solids, SketchUp model from the solids, calcs (incl. modal, LC6, LC7, rebuilt C4/C6/C11/C12, retracted-tip reach), checks (exact OCC clash, SUPPORT-PATH, min clearance >= 25 moving/fixed), MOTION SWEEPS (gate swing, guide park slides, saw traverse on every guide, form load from above, roll on the flip trestle), motion videos, risk assessment (EN ISO 12100 / PUWER), FAT sheet, operator manual + labels.
